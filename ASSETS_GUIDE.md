## 💱 Forex - High Volatility Pairs ONLY

**Tier 1: MAXIMUM Volatility (Use These!)**
```
GBP/USD  (GBPUSD=X)  ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐  Brexit, BoE decisions
USD/JPY  (USDJPY=X)  ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐  Risk-on/off, yield diffs
AUD/USD  (AUDUSD=X)  ⭐⭐⭐⭐   China data, commodities
EUR/USD  (EURUSD=X)  ⭐⭐⭐⭐   ECB policy, inflation
```

**Tier 2: Medium-High Volatility**
```
NZD/USD  (NZDUSD=X)  ⭐⭐⭐
USD/CAD  (USDCAD=X)  ⭐⭐⭐  Oil correlated
GBP/JPY  (GBPJPY=X)  ⭐⭐⭐⭐  Carry trade
EUR/JPY  (EURJPY=X)  ⭐⭐⭐⭐  Carry trade
```

**Avoid (Low Volatility):**
- USD/CHF (flight-to-safety, moves slowly)
- EUR/GBP (too correlated)
- AUD/JPY (inconsistent)

**Why Forex Pairs?**
- 24/5 trading (ideal for swing trade D1)
- Central bank decisions = massive moves
- Risk-on/off events = 100+ pips daily
- No stock market hours limitation

**Best Trading Strategy:**
1. Watch for Fed/ECB/BoE announcements
2. Trade 2-4 hours after announcement
3. Ride the trend for 3-7 days
4. Average swing: 200-500 pips
